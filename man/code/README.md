# FACTS-Debias

## Abstract

Large language models (LLMs) often exhibit systematic social biases with respect to sensitive attributes such as gender and race. Recent data-level debiasing methods mitigate such biases by using anti-biased or counterfactual texts as supervision. However, these texts are not pure debiasing signals: besides debiasing-relevant cues, they may contain sensitive-attribute-related semantics that are not themselves bias signals. Directly using them as supervision may therefore reduce stereotypical preferences while unintentionally perturbing non-pejorative factual distinctions that should be preserved. To address this challenge, we propose FACTS-Debias, a fact-anchored framework that purifies anti-biased supervision for fact-preserving LLM debiasing. FACTS-Debias uses factual anchor texts to learn a representation decomposition that separates anti-bias-specific information from sensitive-attribute-related but bias-irrelevant semantics. It then converts the extracted anti-bias-specific signal into reusable text-based supervision for downstream LLM debiasing. Experiments on five LLMs and three gender groups show that our method consistently achieves stronger bias reduction with better factual-distinction preservation than representative prior debiasing methods. On LLaMA2-7B, averaged across three gender groups, FACTS-Debias obtains a 2.19× larger BPR_b reduction while incurring only 26.0% of the BPR_f degradation of the strongest baseline in our comparison.
